## 6. Global consistency pass

These changes should be applied consistently rather than only at the cited lines:

1. Use spaces in measurements: `6 m`, `10 cm`, `50 m`, `180–210 cm`, rather than `6m`, `10cm`, `50m`, or `180/210cm`.
2. Use one spelling convention. The file mostly follows US English (`armor`, `color`, `meter`); retain that convention throughout.
3. Standardize technical terms: use `Magic Check`, `Magic Critical Success`, `Magic Critical Failure`, `Saving Throw`, `Attack Roll`, `Hit Points`, and `Defense` consistently.
4. Choose one capitalization policy for technical terms. Capitalize them when they are formal game terms, and use lowercase when they are ordinary prose.
5. Standardize monster stat-block labels and punctuation: `Melee Weapon Attack`, `Ranged Weapon Attack`, singular `Hit:` for one attack, and complete sentences where possible.
6. Replace ordinary three-period ellipses (`...`) with `\ldots` or `\dots`, and avoid ellipses where a complete sentence is clearer.
7. Prefer `a/an` and `and` constructions that sound natural in English; avoid literal or telegraphic forms such as `Magic Proficiency requirement 6`, `Usable a number of times`, and `Costs 2 Actions` when they occur in normal prose.
8. Keep the point of view consistent within a rule block. The manual alternates between second person (`you`) and third person (`the character`); either can work, but the same paragraph should not switch without a reason.
9. Review articles before glossary definitions. Several entries currently begin with `is`, `indicates`, or a lowercase noun after the bold label; rewrite them as complete sentences.
10. Review all `\hyperlink`, `\label`, and `\index` arguments separately. Some still contain Italian names, spaces, or different English terminology. These are mainly LaTeX/navigation issues, but they can expose duplicated or untranslated visible text.

## 7. Recommended order of work

1. Apply the certain typos and duplicated-word corrections.
2. Apply the sentence rewrites that repair incomplete or misleading English.
3. Apply the global terminology and measurement pass.
4. Recompile with XeLaTeX and inspect the glossary, tables, monster stat blocks, and hyperlinks, because some labels are used both as navigation anchors and as visible text.
